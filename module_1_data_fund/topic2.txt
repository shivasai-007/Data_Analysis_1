What is Data Collection?
Definition
Data collection is the process of gathering, measuring, and recording information from various sources to answer 
questions, solve problems, or support decision-making.

Simple Def:
Data collection is the process of gathering raw data from different sources for analysis.

Why is Data Collection Important?

Imagine you want to analyze customer purchasing behavior.

If your data is:

❌ Incomplete
❌ Incorrect
❌ Outdated

Then your analysis will also be inaccurate.

This is why people say:

"Garbage In, Garbage Out (GIGO)."

Poor-quality data leads to poor-quality insights.

Data Cleaning Cycle

Data cleaning (also called Data Cleansing or Data Preprocessing) is the process of detecting and correcting errors, 
inconsistencies, and inaccuracies in raw data before analysis.

Simple Definition:
Data cleaning is the process of making raw data accurate, complete, and consistent so it can be used for analysis.
